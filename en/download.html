Alice for Set-top Box

Connect the Alice device to any television using a standard HDMI cable. No technical expertise needed — just plug in and follow the on-screen setup.

1
Download the software file below onto a USB drive or follow the device's on-screen instructions.
2
Connect the Alice set-top box to your TV via HDMI and plug in the power cable.
3
Follow the on-screen setup wizard — takes about 5 minutes.
4
Invite family members to connect through the Alice app on their phones.

Alice for Mobile

The Alice mobile app works on both iOS (iPhone/iPad) and Android devices. Family members can install it too, to stay connected with their senior loved ones.

1
Download Alice from the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).
2
Create your Alice account or sign in if you already have one.
3
Set up your profile and preferences — Alice personalizes to your needs.
4
Connect with family members and your Alice set-top box if you have one.
