Common challenges seniors face today:

  • Chronic diseases and neurodegenerative disorders like Alzheimer's
  • Mobility limitations and fall risks
  • Mental health — loneliness, depression, and anxiety
  • Cognitive decline, including dementia
  • Complex medication management
  • Nutrition and weight management
  • Access to healthcare and telehealth
  • Risk of elder abuse and financial scams
  • Struggles keeping up with rapid technology changes
  • Financial insecurity and limited access to financial services
